PrintMyOsm – Upload tiles

Welcome to PrintMyOsm!

PrintMyOsm is a tool for generating printable maps from OpenStreetMap data.

  1. Choose a polygon of terrain to be included in the map
  2. Use printmyosm.py to download the tiles that make up the polygon
  3. Upload the tiles here
  4. Print this document by pressing Ctrl+P
